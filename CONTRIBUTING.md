# Contributing

Additions welcome. The bar is low in spirit and firm in practice:

1. **Alive** — active repo, not archived, commits within the last year.
2. **Documented** — a README that explains what it does and how to use it.
3. **Actually about pi** — built for pi/oh-my-pi, or a cross-agent tool with
   explicit, documented pi support (those go under "Friends of π").
4. **One line** — `- [name](url) — what it does and why it's useful.`
   Descriptions state value, not adjectives.
5. **Alphabetical** — within its section. π doesn't play favorites.

## How

Fork, add your entry to the right section of `README.md`, open a PR.
One entry (or one coherent group) per PR. If a repo fits two sections,
pick the one a searching human would try first — no duplicate links.

## Removals

Dead, archived, or abandoned entries get pruned. If your project moved,
open a PR pointing to the new home — we'd rather redirect than delete.

## Self-promotion

Fine. Half of every awesome list is self-promotion; just meet the bar.
